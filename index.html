ProfessorDM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ProfessorDM is an experimental capstone project that connects with Instagram to receive and process messages for testing and development purposes.

Information We Receive

When an authorized Instagram account interacts with ProfessorDM, we may receive information provided through the Instagram API, including account identifiers, message content, message metadata, and supported media.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate, test, evaluate, and improve the ProfessorDM platform, including message processing, AI-assisted workflows, moderation, response handling, and system reliability testing.

Data Sharing

ProfessorDM does not sell personal information. Information may be processed through service providers required to operate the platform, such as cloud, database, AI, and messaging infrastructure providers.

Data Retention and Security

We aim to retain only the information required for development, testing, troubleshooting, and approved project workflows. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored information.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with ProfessorDM by contacting the project team using the contact information below.
